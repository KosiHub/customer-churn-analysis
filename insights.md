# Customer Churn Analysis – Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The analysis covers 7,043 telecom customers and identifies several customer
characteristics associated with elevated churn.

The overall churn rate is 26.54%, with 1,869 customers having churned.

A particularly high-risk segment was identified among customers with
month-to-month contracts, fiber optic internet, and tenure of 12 months or
less. This segment has a churn rate of 70.20%.

## Key Findings

### 1. Contract type is strongly associated with churn

Month-to-month customers have a churn rate of 42.71%, compared with:

- One-year contracts: 11.27%
- Two-year contracts: 2.83%

Customers with longer contracts show substantially lower churn.

### 2. New customers are particularly vulnerable

Customers with 0–12 months of tenure have a churn rate of 47.44%.

Churn decreases substantially as customer tenure increases, reaching only
6.61% among customers with 61–72 months of tenure.

### 3. Fiber optic customers have elevated churn

Fiber optic customers have a churn rate of 41.89%, compared with:

- DSL: 18.96%
- No internet service: 7.40%

Fiber optic customers remain associated with higher churn even after
considering contract type.

### 4. Electronic check customers have the highest churn

Customers paying by electronic check have a churn rate of 45.29%.

This compares with:

- Mailed check: 19.11%
- Automatic bank transfer: 16.71%
- Automatic credit card: 15.24%

### 5. Higher monthly charges are associated with higher churn

Churned customers pay an average of $74.44 per month compared with $61.27
for retained customers.

Customers paying under $30 per month have a churn rate of only 9.80%, while
customers in higher price groups show churn rates above 26%.

### 6. A high-risk customer segment was identified

Customers meeting all three conditions:

- Month-to-month contract
- Fiber optic internet
- Tenure of 12 months or less

represent 916 customers.

Of these customers, 643 churned, resulting in a churn rate of 70.20%.

This is approximately 2.6 times the overall company churn rate.

## Business Recommendations

1. Target new month-to-month fiber customers with early retention campaigns.
2. Encourage customers to move from month-to-month contracts to longer-term contracts.
3. Investigate customer satisfaction, pricing, and service quality among fiber optic customers.
4. Investigate why electronic check customers show substantially higher churn.
5. Consider targeted incentives for high-risk customers during their first 12 months.

## Important Note

The analysis identifies associations between customer characteristics and
churn. These results should not be interpreted as evidence that these
characteristics directly cause customers to churn.
